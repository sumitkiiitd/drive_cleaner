🧹 Drive Duplicate File Cleaner

Find and remove duplicate files in your Google Drive

This app runs entirely in your browser. It signs you in with Google, scans your whole Drive, groups exact duplicates by their content checksum across photos, videos, PDFs, and other documents, and lets you review each group before doing anything. Deleted files are moved to your Drive Trash — nothing is permanently deleted, so you can always restore a mistake.

  1. Click Sign in with Google and grant Drive access. (A default Client ID is already configured — open Settings only if you want to use your own.)
  2. Click Scan for duplicates, filter by file type, review the groups, and trash the ones you don't need — individually or in bulk.
