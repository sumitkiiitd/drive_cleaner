🧹 Drive Duplicate Photo Cleaner

Find and remove duplicate photos in your Google Drive

This app runs entirely in your browser. It signs you in with Google, scans your Drive for image files, groups exact duplicates by their content checksum, and lets you review each group before doing anything. Deleted files are moved to your Drive Trash — nothing is permanently deleted, so you can always restore a mistake.

  1. Open Settings and paste in your own Google OAuth Client ID (one-time setup, see README).
  2. Click Sign in with Google and grant Drive access.
  3. Click Scan for duplicates, review the groups, and trash the ones you don't need.

No Google OAuth Client ID is configured yet. Open Settings to add one. See the project README for step-by-step instructions on creating a free Client ID in Google Cloud Console.
